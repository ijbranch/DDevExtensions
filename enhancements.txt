DDevExtensions - Potential Enhancements
=======================================

High-Value Enhancements
-----------------------

1. Code Metrics Dashboard
   Extend the Build Time Statistics feature to include:
   - Historical build time trends (graph over multiple builds)
     COMPLEXITY: Medium | EFFORT: Moderate | BENEFIT: Medium-High
   - Export to JSON/HTML reports
     COMPLEXITY: Low | EFFORT: Low | BENEFIT: Medium

2. Unit Dependency Visualization
   Enhance the Dependency Viewer with:
   - Graphical dependency diagram (like a node graph)
     COMPLEXITY: High | EFFORT: Significant | BENEFIT: High
   - Export to DOT/GraphViz format
     COMPLEXITY: Low | EFFORT: Low | BENEFIT: Medium-High
   - Export circular references analysis to file (CSV/TXT)
     COMPLEXITY: Low | EFFORT: Low | BENEFIT: Medium-High
     NOTE: Useful for analyzing large numbers of circular refs externally
   - Layer violation detection (configurable architecture rules)
     COMPLEXITY: Medium-High | EFFORT: Moderate | BENEFIT: High
   - Respect project conditional defines ({$IFDEF}) in dependency analysis
     COMPLEXITY: Medium | EFFORT: Moderate | BENEFIT: High
     STATUS: IMPLEMENTED in v3.5
     Reads project defines from .dproj, evaluates {$IFDEF}, {$IFNDEF},
     {$IF Defined(...)}, {$ELSE}, {$ELSEIF} with full OR/AND support.
     "Respect conditional compilation" checkbox added to options.
